## Hi

I am a software developer, computer programmer, coder... whatever you wanna
call it. I use this site as my attempt at blogging about things I learn,
projects I'm currently working on, and ideas about different things that
interest me.

Over the years, I've gone deeper into different technical topics, such as
`web`, `mobile`, `backend`, `databases`, `concurrency`, `compilers`,
`interpreters`, `runtimes`, `networking`, `cryptography`, `operating systems`,
and a few other things.

I believe in free and open software, privacy, and autonomy. I oppose the
concentration of power, mass surveillance, the constant commodification and
quantification of our lives, and AI. You can read more about me in
[/about](/about).

If you'd like to talk about anything or have any questions, you can always
write to me at [email](mailto:hi@claridelune.com).
